===== page 2 =====
Prologue: Our Competitive Challenges
1. “Structural Repricing and Extreme Costs”
– Price: “soaring inflation, skyrocketing [lodging] rates (30% jump over 5-
yr span), high local taxes, and steep airfares.”
– Value Redefinition: Travelers prioritize places “that offer clear
demonstrable value.”
2. “Growing Exhaustion Over Overtourism”
– US travelers tired of mass tourism; in Hawai‘i, overcrowding and
resident resentment are growing concerns
3. “Intense Global and Domestic Competition”
– Many travelers now plan long-haul trips; Hawai‘i faces aggressive price
competition from international competition
– Weather/Environment: “Destinations that don’t effectively manage their
environmental footprint or offer eco-friendly value risk losing visitors to
emerging alternatives.”
Source: AI summary of 2026 “MMGY Portrait of American Travelers” Hawai‘i results

Part 1 Major Take-Aways
• Our rate has mushroomed, now very high
• Still, changes in TAT/GET rates (alone) not
strongly correlated to most tourism outcomes
• But tourism now (a) weaker than before
Pandemic, and (b) headed south – and we’re
not used to downturns in “normal” timeframe
• Evidence that visitor concern about expense
(if not taxes alone) is a major (but not only)
issue negatively affecting visitor perceptions

Other Complexities
- Broadening base of TAT applications
(timeshares, STRs, resort fees)

by visitors (e.g., surcharge taxes on
rental cars, tour vehicles, car sharing)

owned attractions (hiking, botanical, etc.)

===== page 26 =====
Conclusion: Part 2 Implications

For “tourism competitiveness,” must consider
designated uses, not just level of TAT rate.

And that’s highly political!

While TAT tweaks may have small actual tourism
effects, possible symbolic effects of demonstrating
support via recommendations like:
• System for annual monitoring/publishing of total
revenues (incl. fees and lesser taxes) from tourism
• Recommended percentage ranges (floors/ceilings)
for uses – a%-b% for marketing; c%-d% for
management; e%-f% for tourism infrastructure; g%-
h% for general society
