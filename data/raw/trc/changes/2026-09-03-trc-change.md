PRESENTATION BY DR. DYLAN MOORE REGARDING THE UNIVERSITY OF
HAWAI’I ECONOMIC RESEARCH ORGANIZATION (UHERO) PROPOSAL FOR
TRC PROJECTS; QUESTIONS; AND DISCUSSION:
Dr. Karacaovali started the presentation with providing a brief introduction for Dr. Moore, a
public finance economist at the University of Hawaii Manoa.
Dr. Moore then introduced himself and acknowledged Dr. Nicolas Lacoste who was not at the
meeting but will be joining UHERO in the fall to assist Dr. Moore.
Dr. Moore provided an overview of what he thinks his role would be as a consultant to the TRC
based on the conversations with Ms. Chock, Mr. Fukunaga, and Dr. Karacaovali.
Dr. Moore said his knowledge and views of how tax policy works can contribute to the TRC by
providing a better understanding of the tax system and what sort of alternatives might be possible
and what would happen if the State was to pursue an alternative tax structure compared to the
present tax structure.
Dr. Moore continued to state that the central focus will be the notion of tax competitiveness
which would address the following:
1. What sort of public policies might lead people to leave the State
2. What sorts of public policies are going to make people want to remain in the State
3. What tax policies might encourage business activity in the State
4. What sorts of public policies or tax policies might lead businesses to leave the State
