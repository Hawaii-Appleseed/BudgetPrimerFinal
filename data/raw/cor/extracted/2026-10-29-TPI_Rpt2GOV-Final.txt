Dear Governor Green:
In its meeting on October 29, 2026, the Council on Revenues (the “Council”) retained the
Hawai‘i Total Personal Income (TPI) forecast for calendar year (CY) 2026 at 2.0%. The Council raised
its calendar year 2027 TPI forecast from 2.0% to 4.5%.
The Council received information from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) that the
CY 2025 TPI figures released by BEA assumed settlement payments being made to households in the
fourth quarter of 2025, related to the 2023 Maui wildfires. BEA advised that these payments were
recorded in the fourth quarter of 2025 based on accrual accounting principles and the timing of the
final settlement agreement. This led to a higher than anticipated preliminary TPI growth rate in 2025.
The Council had initially assumed that the settlement funds would enter the economy over several
years as payments were actually distributed. After receiving clarification from BEA, however, it
became clear that the agency had already recognized the settlement's impact in 2025 personal income
figures. Most importantly for the Council's deliberations, BEA indicated that it does not anticipate
additional impacts from these settlement payments being recorded in TPI figures for 2026 or later
years. The Council took into account the implications of the elevated 2025 growth rate and the
likelihood of slower measured growth in 2026 due to the absence of a similar transfer payment event in
revising its TPI forecast for 2026 and 2027 based on the recent BEA information.
