Dear Governor Green:
In its meeting on July 30, 2026, the Council on Revenues (the “Council”) lowered the Hawai‘i
Total Personal Income (TPI) forecast for calendar year (CY) 2026 to 2.0%. The Council set its
calendar year 2027 TPI forecast at 2.0% as well.
The Council noted that the preliminary CY 2025 TPI figures released in June 2026 by the U.S.
Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) assumed settlement payments being made to households in the
fourth quarter of 2025, related to the 2023 Maui wildfires. This led to a higher than anticipated
preliminary TPI growth rate in 2025. The BEA is expected to issue its final 2025 TPI growth rate in
September 2026. The Council recognized that the settlement fund has only been partially funded to
date, and may be disbursed and spread over the next several years in its TPI estimated growth rates for
2026 and 2027.
The updated data for Hawaii’s TPI for calendar years 2021 through 2025, along with the
Council’s current forecasts for 2026 and 2027, are shown below:
